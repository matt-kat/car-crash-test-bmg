User Data Deletion Instructions

This page explains how users of Car Crash Test BMG can request deletion of personal data associated with their use of the game.

Car Crash Test BMG is operated by MK-Play, referred to below as the "Service Provider".

How to Request Deletion of Your Data

If you would like the Service Provider to delete personal data associated with your use of Car Crash Test BMG, please send an email to:

mattgames.group@gmail.com

Please use the following email subject:

Car Crash Test BMG - Data Deletion Request

In your request, please provide enough information for us to identify the data associated with you. This may include, where available:

  • Your Facebook or Meta User ID
  • Your Facebook or Meta Player ID
  • Your in-game player identifier
  • Any other identifier associated with your game account

Please do not send your Facebook password, Meta password, or any other account password.

Requesting Deletion Through Facebook

You may also remove Car Crash Test BMG from your Facebook account through Facebook's Apps and Websites settings.

  1. Open Facebook and go to Settings & Privacy.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Go to Apps and Websites.
  4. Find Car Crash Test BMG.
  5. Select the option to remove the app.
  6. If Facebook provides an option to request deletion of information associated with the app, select that option.

Removing the Application from Facebook may stop future access to information provided through Facebook, but it may not automatically delete information previously transmitted to and stored by the Service Provider.

To ensure that data held by the Service Provider is deleted, you may also contact us directly at mattgames.group@gmail.com.

What Data Will Be Deleted

Depending on how you have used the Application, data associated with your account may include:

  • Facebook or Meta identifiers received by the Application
  • Player or account identifiers
  • Game progress associated with your account, where applicable
  • Information you provided directly to the Service Provider
  • Technical information associated with your account or use of the Application

When a valid deletion request is received, the Service Provider will delete or anonymize personal data that can reasonably be associated with the requesting user and that is under the Service Provider's control.

If We Do Not Store Your Data

In some cases, Car Crash Test BMG may process information temporarily without permanently storing personal data on the Service Provider's servers.

If we determine that we do not hold personal data associated with your request, we will inform you that there is no stored user data available for deletion.

Third-Party Services

Car Crash Test BMG may use third-party services, including Facebook/Meta and Unity.

A deletion request sent to MK-Play applies to personal data controlled by MK-Play. Data independently controlled by third-party providers is subject to those providers' own privacy and data deletion policies.

Data We May Be Required to Retain

In limited circumstances, the Service Provider may retain certain information when required by applicable law, or when reasonably necessary for purposes such as fraud prevention, security, dispute resolution, or compliance with legal obligations.

Any retained information will only be kept for as long as necessary for those purposes.

Processing Time

We aim to process verified data deletion requests within 30 days of receipt, unless a different period is required or permitted by applicable law.

If additional information is reasonably required to identify your data, we may contact you using the email address from which the request was sent.

Confirmation

After your request has been processed, we may send you confirmation that the deletion request has been completed.
